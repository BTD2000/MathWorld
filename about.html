About MathWorld

MathWorld is a platform dedicated to discovering, sharing, and exploring interesting mathematical problems and competitions. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced problem solver, MathWorld aims to help you find new mathematical challenges and opportunities.

Our Purpose

Many interesting mathematical problems and competitions are difficult to discover because they are scattered across different websites and communities. MathWorld aims to bring these resources together in one organized place, making them easier to find and explore.

What We Offer

  • Mathematical Problems: A collection of interesting problems from various sources, covering Algebra, Combinatorics, Geometry, and Number Theory.
  • Contests: Opportunities to participate in mathematical competitions and challenge your problem-solving skills.
  • Solutions: A place to share mathematical solutions and learn from different approaches.

Our Vision

We hope to build a growing mathematical platform where problem solvers can discover new challenges, share their ideas, participate in contests, and explore the beauty of mathematics together.

Mathematical Typesetting

MathWorld supports LaTeX, allowing users to write mathematical expressions, equations, and formulas in a clear and professional format. This makes even complex mathematical notation easier to read and understand.

BBCode Support

MathWorld also supports BBCode, a simple markup system that helps users format their solutions and posts. Users can organize their writing with features such as bold text, italics, lists, and other formatting options.
