Some of my favourite formulas

Basel problem

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^2}=\frac{1}{1^2}+\frac{1}{2^2}+\frac{1}{3^2}+\cdots=\frac{\pi^2}{6}$$

Euler product formula

$$\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^s}=\prod_{p\in\mathbb{P}}\frac{1}{1-p^{-s}}$$

Euler's identity

$$e^{i\pi}+1=0$$
